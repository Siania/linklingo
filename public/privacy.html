Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 March 2026 · GDPR (EEA/UK)

This policy describes how Linklingo (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use this website and web application. We do not operate a social network, we do not require an account, and we do not collect personal data for marketing, advertising profiling, or sale.

Who is responsible

The operator of the deployment you are using is the data controller for processing described here. The app is published as Linklingo; the footer of the main page names the maintainer.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to register or log in.
  • We do not maintain a customer database of visitors for marketing.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or marketing analytics cookies on this service.

Generating posts and About text

When you choose to generate a LinkedIn-style post or an About section, the text you provide is sent to our server over HTTPS and forwarded to an AI provider (for example OpenAI, Google Gemini, or xAI, depending on how the server is configured) solely to produce the output you requested. That processing is necessary to deliver the feature. The AI providers act as subprocessors; their terms and privacy notices apply to that processing step.

We do not use your prompts to build a behavioural profile about you or to send you marketing.

History on your device

If you use the history/backlog feature, entries are stored only in your browser (for example localStorage) on your device. That data is not uploaded to us unless you run a generation request that includes that content in the request.

CV and file uploads

PDF text is usually extracted in your browser; if that fails, the file may be sent once to extract text in memory. We do not keep uploaded files as permanent storage. Word and plain-text flows are described in the in-app notice next to the upload area.

Optional server logging (operators)

On a given deployment, the server operator may enable technical request logging for security or administration. When enabled, limited data (such as a timestamp, truncated request content, and IP address) may be written to a log file. By default this logging is turned off. It does not change the fact that we do not use such data for marketing.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set cookies for advertising. Your browser or hosting infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms needed to serve the site.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on Article 6(1)(b) (processing necessary for the service you request) for generation requests, and on Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests) where applicable for security and abuse prevention, balanced against your rights.

Retention

We do not retain your text as part of a user profile on our side. Optional operator logs, if enabled, should be rotated or deleted according to the operator’s policy.

International transfers

AI providers may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area. Those providers typically use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as described in their documentation.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to data portability, under the GDPR. To exercise these rights, contact the operator of the site you are using. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children

The service is intended for professional use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change.
